# Your Move for Jira

**Every task. A clear next move.**

Your Move is an Atlassian Forge app that separates responsibility for delivering a Jira issue from the person who needs to perform its next action. An issue can have several independent Balls. Handoffs retain context and history, and recipients receive a personal Inbox notice.
